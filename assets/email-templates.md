# Email Templates

## Invoice Delivery
Subject: Invoice #{number} — {amount} due {date}
Body: Hi {name},\n\nPlease find your invoice attached.\n\nAmount: {amount}\nDue: {date}\nPay online: {link}\n\nThank you for your business.

## Follow-up After Meeting
Subject: Great speaking with you — next steps
Body: Hi {name},\n\nThanks for your time today. As discussed:\n\n{bullet_points}\n\nI'll {next_action} by {date}. Let me know if you have questions.

## Payment Reminder
Subject: Friendly reminder: Invoice #{number} is overdue
Body: Hi {name},\n\nThis is a friendly reminder that invoice #{number} for {amount} was due on {date}.\n\nPay online: {link}\n\nPlease let us know if you have any questions.
